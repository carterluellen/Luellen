    <p class="eyebrow">Perspective</p>
    <h2>Questions rarely fit inside one discipline.</h2>
    <p>My academic interests bring together biology, chemistry, and space science. I’m especially drawn to research that connects molecular and material properties with the behavior of larger biological systems.</p>
    <p>Through laboratory research, I’ve developed interests in extracellular matrix biology, human gastric organoid models, and the use of microscopy to characterize biological materials.</p>
    <p>I’m motivated by the process of learning a system deeply: understanding its underlying chemistry, selecting methods that can reveal its structure, and interpreting what the results can—and cannot—tell us.</p>
